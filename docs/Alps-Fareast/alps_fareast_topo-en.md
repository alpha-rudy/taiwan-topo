### Famous Peaks

This region lies at the far eastern edge of the Alps, running from the Kamnik-Savinja Alps and Karawanks of Slovenia in the south, north to Austria's Northern Limestone Alps, all the way to the outskirts of Vienna:

* Grintovec (2,558m): the highest peak of the Kamnik-Savinja Alps.
* Hochstuhl / Stol (2,236m): the highest peak of the Karawanks, straddling the Austria-Slovenia border.
* Hochschwab (2,277m): a limestone plateau massif in Styria, an important habitat for wild chamois.
* Hochtor (2,369m): the highest peak of Gesäuse National Park, known for its steep climbing routes.
* Schneeberg (2,076m): the highest peak of Lower Austria, the easternmost 2,000m summit in the Alps.
* Rax (2,007m): neighboring Schneeberg, and the most popular local mountain for day trips from nearby Vienna.

### Famous Trekking Routes

* Slovenska planinska pot (Slovenian Mountain Trail), northern section:
  * Passes through the Kamnik Alps and along the Karawanks ridgeline, linking Slovenia's most rugged high-mountain terrain.
* Kamnik Alps ridge traverse:
  * Threads along the jagged ridgeline above the Logar Valley, known for steep terrain and magnificent views.
* Schneeberg & Rax day hikes near Vienna:
  * High-mountain routes reachable as a day trip from Vienna, with a century-old rack railway or cable car offering easy access to the summit.

### Famous Sights

* Logar Valley (Logarska dolina): one of Slovenia's most beautiful glacial U-shaped valleys, its floor ringed by waterfalls and sheer mountain walls.
* Velika Planina: a high mountain pasture at roughly 1,600m, preserving a distinctive settlement of traditional herders' huts.
* Gesäuse National Park: famous for the rugged gorge and whitewater carved through limestone by the Enns River.
* Schneebergbahn: Austria's highest-altitude rack railway, a popular attraction just outside Vienna.
* Erzberg: a giant open-pit iron mine cut into stepped terraces, a distinctive industrial landscape of the region.

### Historical Events

* 1897 - Schneebergbahn opens: one of the world's foremost high-mountain rack railways of its era, it let Viennese city-dwellers reach the Alps with ease, launching the Austro-Hungarian-era vogue for "mountaineering for the masses."
* Early 20th century - The Rax climbing ethics debate: legendary Austrian climber Paul Preuss championed "free climbing" around Rax and Schneeberg, opposing excessive reliance on pitons; this sparked the famous "Mauerhakenstreit" (piton controversy), which profoundly shaped the development of modern free-climbing ethics.
* Erzberg's thousand-year mining history: iron ore has been mined here since the Middle Ages, making it one of the few mines in Europe worked continuously for over a millennium, long underpinning Austria's steel industry.
* During WWII, the Velika Planina area was an operating ground for Slovenian partisan forces; the traditional herders' hut settlement was damaged by the fighting on several occasions and rebuilt afterward.
