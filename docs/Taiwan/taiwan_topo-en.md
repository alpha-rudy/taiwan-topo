MOI.OSM - Taiwan TOPO
=====================

MOI.OSM - Taiwan TOPO v__version__, released every Thursday

For hiking and geocaching on Garmin / Android / iOS!

* Related Links
  * RudyMap Mirror Selector (Cedric Shih)
    * https://rudymap.tw/
    * https://rudymap.tw/app (dedicated mobile offline app)
  * Info pages:
    * https://moi.kcwu.csie.org/taiwan_topo-en.html (mirror kcwu)

  * Discussion (Facebook Group): https://www.facebook.com/groups/taiwan.topo

## About

RudyMap (MOI.OSM) is released every Thursday, using that day's OSM data. Hikers only need to download it before a trip, or update it once a season - there's no need to update every week.

OSM is free, open-source map data, painstakingly co-edited by the dedicated mappers of [OpenStreetMap Taiwan](http://openstreetmap.tw). Keeping the data accurate depends on hikers working together to maintain it; if you're interested, visit the OSM Taiwan website to learn how to report or fix errors on the map.

Contour lines are generated mainly from the "Ministry of the Interior 20-meter grid digital terrain model data" ([data.moi.gov.tw](https://data.moi.gov.tw/)), accurate enough for general mountaineering reference. Errors may still exist, and hikers are welcome to report corrections for everyone's discussion and reference. This digital map suits all kinds of hiking activities in Taiwan, but for multi-day mountain trips it's still recommended to print a paper map as backup ([map generator](https://map.happyman.idv.tw/~happyman/twmap3)).

The style was jointly designed by Beisan and myself to suit hiking and geocaching. Suggestions are welcome on the [RudyMap Facebook Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/taiwan.topo).

RudyMap may be freely downloaded and shared with others (please use this page's link to distribute it). Please note in particular that the map may contain errors, and the author cannot guarantee its accuracy. Users are solely responsible for any loss (including injury) that may result from using this map.
